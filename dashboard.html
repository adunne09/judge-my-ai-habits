TL;DR

    Want advice? Ask your own agent

    This page only describes your habits. It never gives advice, because the data behind it is anonymized on purpose: no titles, prompts or folders. Recommendations need that context, so they stay on your machine. Paste this prompt into the AI agent you already use; it reads your local database and suggests how to raise your leverage and complexity.

    Prompt for your agent
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    What is complexity?
    
    
    Every session gets a complexity level from 0 to 6. It answers one question: how much work would it take skilled professionals, working without AI, to do everything you asked for? Here is the scale, and how your sessions spread across it.
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    How you put AI to work
    
    
    The second score, leverage (0 to 5), is about how the work was handed over, not how big it was. A tiny task can be delegated and a huge one can be steered by hand.
    
    
    
    
    Click a level in either table to list its sessions below.

    More requests, more tokens?
