    What is “complexity”?

    The 0–6 scale every session is scored on.

    Complexity asks: how much work would it take skilled professionals, working without AI, to do everything you asked for in this session?

    your sessions
    0a couple of minutesa quick question, answered on the spot
    1under an houra small task one person finishes quickly
    2a few hoursa focused chunk of real work
    3about a daya full day of skilled work
    4several daysa small project, days rather than hours
    5weeks for one personweeks of full-time work for one skilled person
    6months for a teamwhat a team of specialists would need months to build
    A session's score is a weighted average across these levels, so 1.84 means “between level 1 and level 2”. The companion score, leverage (0–5), asks a different question: how did you put the AI to work — from answering from memory up to running an autonomous agent loop.
